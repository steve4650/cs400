Depth or Breadth First - Complexities

Prim's Algorithm - minimal spanning tree for WEIGHTED trees.
- note: relies on PRIORITY QUEUE

Kruskal's algorithm
- assume all nodes unvisited
- kruskal(V, E)
  - sort edge list
  - have set of subsets of V
  - in increasing order:
    - pop minimal edge (m,n)
    - skip of there is a subset already containing m and n
    - build/combine subsets otherwise
  - at the end, the subset your left with is a MST

Prim and Kruskal are both O(E*log(V))
